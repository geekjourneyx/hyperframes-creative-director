# Film brief

## Content and goal
Source links or supplied materials, verified claims, audience, key message and intended response.

## Creative direction
Concept, visual language, narrative and what makes this topic recognizable.

Use these decisions when helpful; infer routine choices. These body notes add no required machine fields or extra approval stage.

| Decision | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| memory_point | What should the audience retain? |
| proof | Source and claim; distinguish real evidence from conceptual illustration. |
| visual_idea | Which content-specific relationship becomes visible? |
| visual_language | Brand palette/type, light/material, composition and motion character. |
| selected_refs | Zero or more references, adaptations/combinations, or free-text custom approach and its purpose. Empty is valid; no catalog membership required. |
| motion_risks | What stills cannot prove and the cheapest meaningful motion probe. |

See [selection examples](../references/technique-selection.md#brief-examples). Recipe and music anchors are optional. Record platform/crop and reading constraints with the chosen format.

## Copy and shots
Final on-screen copy, optional narration, approximate timing, intended actions and transitions. Default language: English; record any requested override.

## Sound and media
Music direction, rhythm, voice/sound effects, media sources and rights considerations. State any intentional silence or black intervals.
