Start with the content's meaning and communication job. Read [technique selection](technique-selection.md) when a choice is uncertain. Use the [complete vocabulary index](craft-index.md) to discover related mechanisms, not to fill a storyboard. Load only useful families.

The twelve detailed entries are a starting tranche, not the limits of the vocabulary or the agent. Reuse an apt mechanism, adapt a partial fit, combine complementary mechanisms, invent from the topic, or proceed without a reference. No technique quota, closed enum, default dark palette, compulsory novelty or separate invention approval.

Read [anti-patterns](anti-patterns.md) for symptoms and repairs, [recipes](recipes.md) for optional combinations and [showreel index](showreel-index.md) for paired source studies. Source studies are authored; preview/export/listening verification is pending. A source link is not proof of a quality win.
