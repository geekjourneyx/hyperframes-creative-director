## Original showreel recovery

Original source/video and rights were not available for this implementation. The [index](craft-index.md#unavailable-showreel) preserves all unresolved identifiers. Reported 30-second duration, ~2-second segments and 26-second END are unverified issue descriptions. No specific S-number mapping or exact TR names are inferred.

After inspecting accessible originals, record aliases, source commit, actual timecodes, rights and a product-specific use/avoid explanation. Publish previews only to a project-owned stable location with content hash/version; local reproduction must not depend on downloading them. New dictionary pages do not automatically become recommended product shots.
