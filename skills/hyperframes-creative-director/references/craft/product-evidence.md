# Product Evidence

Status: original guidance and paired source authored; integration, visual inspection and listening pending. The `strong` and `weak` names identify design intentions, not observed reviewer scores. [Study setup](../../examples/craft-studies/README.md) · [dictionary](../showreel-index.md).

## evidence-reveal

1. **Purpose:** Connect a visible action to its truthful result.
2. **Use / prerequisites:** Use supplied screenshots/capture and documented capabilities. The shipped example is labeled illustrative, never a real UI claim.
3. **Avoid when:** Do not invent UI, conceal time edits or show decorative cards as proof.
4. **Controls:** Retain source identity through movement; reveal the changed region, then hold readable evidence. Keep labels outside moving masks.
5. **Matched contrast:** A labeled sample list of three sources resolves into an action list; weak sweeps the same content past the viewer and obscures it.
6. **Implementation:** [product-evidence.mjs](../../examples/craft-studies/scenes/product-evidence.mjs). DOM illustrative cards; replace with licensed real capture in a film, preserving aspect and readable crop.
7. **Verification / cost:** Inspect the source/result relationship and small text; continuous playback establishes whether the audience can follow it. Low cost until real media is introduced.
8. **Sources / evidence:** #6 compare; #16 cards/terminal; #18 traceable numbers. Issue proposals are traceable in [the index](../craft-index.md); these new examples remain unverified hypotheses about craft outcomes.

## result-comparison

1. **Purpose:** Make a supported difference inspectable.
2. **Use / prerequisites:** Use matched inputs, conditions, scale and a sourced comparison.
3. **Avoid when:** Avoid unmeasured multipliers, changing both design and claim between variants, or ending a number roll mid-value.
4. **Controls:** Keep a stable comparison axis; label before/after and units; freeze final results long enough to read.
5. **Matched contrast:** The same illustrative source/action labels stay aligned; weak changes scale and keeps moving during the hold. No speed claim is made.
6. **Implementation:** [product-evidence.mjs](../../examples/craft-studies/scenes/product-evidence.mjs). The product-evidence module uses a persistent source list beside its sample output.
7. **Verification / cost:** Inspect before/after at the same time and size; verify real claims separately. Lower cost than a spatial comparison when depth adds no meaning.
8. **Sources / evidence:** #6 morph/compare; #16 stats/wall. Issue proposals are traceable in [the index](../craft-index.md); these new examples remain unverified hypotheses about craft outcomes.
