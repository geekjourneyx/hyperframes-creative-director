# Contextual negative guidance

Diagnose a viewer consequence, then repair it. Severity applies to the specific failure, not the underlying medium. Text-only, bright, static and 2D films can be excellent. Sources: issue [#6](https://github.com/geekjourneyx/hyperframes-creative-director/issues/6), [#15](https://github.com/geekjourneyx/hyperframes-creative-director/issues/15), [#18](https://github.com/geekjourneyx/hyperframes-creative-director/issues/18) and the user's direction. These are design hypotheses pending comparative evaluation, except factual/evidence correctness requirements already in the workflow.

## Correctness failures

| Symptom | Consequence / likely cause | Repair | Valid exception / related reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invented real UI, command, result or efficiency multiplier | Misleads viewers; effects substituted for source evidence. | Obtain actual source and conditions or label a conceptual illustration; remove unsupported numbers. | Clearly labeled fiction is not real product proof. [Evidence](craft/product-evidence.md) |
| Missing glyphs or critical clipping/occlusion | Meaning is lost; wrong font, mask or crop. | Load required font/assets, inspect actual rendered glyphs and reflow; fix masks. | Deliberate cropped decorative type is valid if critical meaning survives. [Typography](craft/typography.md) |
| Required font/texture/WebGL fails silently | Empty or misleading export despite a file existing. | Reject readiness with a diagnostic and repair or explicitly choose a suitable fallback. | A disclosed intentional fallback can preserve the concept; never label missing evidence as present. [Studies](../examples/craft-studies/README.md) |
| Numbers stop mid-roll or ambiguous comparison units | Viewers read a false result; timing/formatting error. | Resolve to sourced final values and hold; show units and matched conditions. | A labeled live intermediate state may be meaningful. [Comparison](craft/product-evidence.md#result-comparison) |
| Brand name replaced by an unsupported localized nickname | Product identity becomes incorrect. | Preserve the supplied official name and approved localization. | Authorized localized identity. [Keyframe review](keyframe-review.md) |
| Contact sheet or tool flag claimed as playback/listening/approval | QA evidence is false; inference substituted for observation. | Record actual inspected ranges and artifacts; mark missing observations unverified. | No exception for fabricated evidence. [Final review](final-review.md) |

## Strong creative cautions

| Symptom | Consequence / likely cause | Repair | Valid exception / related reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Isolated typewriter slogan is the main promotional idea | Weak composition and little semantic transformation; animation used as decoration. | Build hierarchy and a visual relationship; reveal whole meaningful units and hold. | Real typing, command execution, dialogue or a purposeful narrative reveal. [Typography study](craft/typography.md) |
| Unreadable module/feature wall | Apparent richness replaces comprehensible evidence. | Select a meaningful crop, establish hierarchy, then hold the result. | A wall can establish scale if not offered as readable feature proof. [Evidence study](craft/product-evidence.md) |
| Generic Tron/neon tunnel, random transitions | Topic becomes interchangeable; technique chosen before idea. | Derive space and transitions from actual relationships. | A network/travel subject may justify a tunnel. [Spatial study](craft/spatial-3d.md) |
| Every element moves; no result hold | Attention fragments; ending cannot be read. | Separate anticipation, arrival and rest; resolve the focal subject. | Deliberate overload can communicate a problem before resolution. [Motion study](craft/motion-sound.md) |
| Bloom/noisy grain hides form or type | Surface loses meaning; effect strength mistaken for quality. | Reduce intensity/area and restore directional light and contact. | Deliberate loss of visibility can serve a narrative interval. [Light study](craft/lighting-materials.md) |
| Unmotivated camera rotation | Viewers lose orientation and product readability. | Tie camera movement to a landmark or information reveal, then settle. | Rotation needed to explain geometry. [Spatial study](craft/spatial-3d.md) |
| Music masks speech; accents on every action | Comprehension and contrast collapse. | Listen in context; adjust level, duck selectively, remove unnecessary accents. | Intentional subjective overload with an understandable payoff. [Sound](craft/motion-sound.md) |
| Reviewing only a contact sheet | Timing, crop transitions and audio defects escape review. | Inspect full frames and continuous exported motion/sound. | Contact sheets remain a useful overview, never a substitute. [Final review](final-review.md) |

## Recipe-local cautions

`washed-crossfade`, `multi-accent` and `flash-spam` from #15 describe risks in a chosen visual language. A muddy dissolve can weaken a crisp reveal; use a cleaner cut or adjust compositing. Multiple unrelated accent colors can destroy hierarchy; organize their roles. Repeated flashes can overpower content and require explicit frequency/area/luminance review. Valid exceptions include a deliberate dissolve, meaningful multicolor categorization and an appropriately reviewed motivated flash. Neither a single accent nor a hard cut is universally superior, and opacity limits do not establish flash safety.

“Glitch after drop,” “crossfade after drop” and “data during breakdown” are contextual cautions, not global bans. Use the intended emotional and informational relationship to decide. Do not substitute arbitrary effect counts for judgment.
