# Optional recipes

Choose governing relationships from the content, brand and available evidence. These are compact starting points, not style packages, required selections or mutually exclusive modes. Custom directions and deliberate combinations are valid. All issue-named alternatives remain in [the index](craft-index.md#recipes).

| Recipe | Good reason to choose it | Governing relationships and source needs | Failure to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cinematic product reveal | Form, material or a physical product carries the promise. | Edge light reveals silhouette; camera reveals construction; a semantic mask opens real evidence. Use supplied product geometry/photos/UI. Dark only if brand, contrast and subject support it. | Empty spectacle, bloom hiding form, orbit hiding UI, assuming a music drop or dark background is mandatory. |
| Bright editorial/product story | Clarity, optimism, language or a readable process carries the idea. | Typographic hierarchy and whitespace guide attention; paper/material shadows establish layers; masks connect source to result. Use real copy and sourced results; reflow vertical layouts. | Treating bright as flat, decorative texture reducing contrast, dense card walls, scaling a landscape layout into unreadable portrait. |
| Screenlife/evidence-led demonstration | An authentic action and its output are persuasive. | Establish real context, show real input, reveal an actual result, hold for inspection. Crop or move through UI with continuity. Genuine typing is valid; silence and restrained sound can emphasize completion. | Fabricated commands/UI, unmeasured speed claims, hiding elapsed time, director effects obscuring the result. |

For light, masks and 3D, read the corresponding [family references](craft-library.md). No recipe exempts false claims, missing assets or unreadable critical evidence. Recipe-local choices such as a hard cut, a limited accent system or a particular camera vocabulary do not become universal bans on dissolves, multiple colors or rotation.
