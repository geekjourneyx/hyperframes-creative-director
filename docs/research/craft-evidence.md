## Noise, overdesign and useful references

The repeated bridge/repository proposals form one dependency decision. Hard technique/camera/transition quotas, compulsory catalog admission, mandatory single-package selection, default dark cinema and universal audio targets lack evidence as general constraints and conflict with the approved creative freedom. A plugin protocol, registry database and aesthetic scoring engine add maintenance without demonstrated reuse.

Keep the underlying mechanisms discoverable. Light, masks and 3D have detailed implementation paths; point clouds, harmonic graphics, easing editors and isometric space remain available when the content needs them. Do not label them inherently inferior or require prior library registration to explore them. Original promo-kit and showreel recovery is a future source audit, not a dependency for the native examples.

The causal hypothesis is that content-specific selection, actionable examples and contextual diagnosis may improve clarity, coherence and craft while reducing unrelated spectacle. The [pilot](../../evals/README.md#craft-upgrade-pilot) tests this at equal budgets. Technical correctness, visual judgment and audience outcomes remain separate. Source completion does not establish effectiveness or authorize issue closures.
